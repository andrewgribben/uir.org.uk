Last updated 2 November 2026.

## Who we are

ÚIR Consulting Ltd is the controller for this website and for any personal data described on this page. We are registered in Northern Ireland, company number NI662497.

## This website does not track you

uir.org.uk sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising scripts, and loads no third-party resources. There is no contact form on the site. All fonts, images, styles and scripts are served from uir.org.uk itself.

## If you email us

If you email us, we process the content of your message and your email address to reply and to deal with your enquiry. We keep that correspondence for as long as we need it to handle the enquiry and to meet our business and legal record-keeping needs, then delete it. Our email is hosted by Google Workspace, which processes messages on our behalf under its own data protection terms.

For privacy questions or to make a data request, email support@uir.org.uk.

## Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you can ask what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, and in many cases ask us to delete it or object to how we use it. To exercise any of these rights, email support@uir.org.uk.

## Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
